# Mesh Seats

**Give each AI helper its own permanent desk: its own memory, its own personality, and its own work history.**

Most AI chats forget everything when you close the window. A seat doesn't. Each AI helper gets one, like an employee gets a desk. It remembers what it learned, keeps the personality you gave it, and keeps a record of every job it did.

## What's in a seat

- **Memory** (`memory.md`): what the helper has learned. You can read and edit it.
- **Personality** (`soul.md`): how it talks, what it cares about, what it won't do.
- **Permissions**: exactly what it's allowed to do and spend.
- **Work history**: a record of every job, with proof.

The seat belongs to you. You can take it with you, or shut it off in one tap.
